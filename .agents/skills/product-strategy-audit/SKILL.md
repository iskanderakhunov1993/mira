# Product Strategy Audit

## Context

Read `docs/product/PRODUCT_OVERVIEW.md`, `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md`, `PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`, `FEATURES.md`, `BUSINESS_MODEL.md`, and `DECISIONS.md`.

## Workflow

1. Define the primary segment and job-to-be-done.
2. State the current value proposition in one sentence.
3. Test whether the product solves a frequent and painful problem.
4. Compare implemented features with the core value.
5. Identify differentiation that is meaningful and defensible.
6. Separate facts, inferences, and unvalidated hypotheses.
7. Recommend what to strengthen, simplify, postpone, or remove.

## Output

- Strategy score out of 10.
- User problem and product thesis.
- What supports positioning.
- Strategic contradictions.
- Differentiation assessment.
- P0/P1/P2 recommendations.
- Three validation experiments.
